About ScanBite

Built for the moment you're standing in the aisle

Most nutrition labels are written for regulators, not shoppers. ScanBite translates every packaged product into plain language the moment you scan it — what's actually in it, what it's good for, and what it isn't.

We're building the database one barcode at a time, cross-checking manufacturer data against open food databases so the score you see is one you can trust.
